Dan Maccarone

Speaking engagements, podcasts and writing.

Business & UX Strategist

Dan Maccarone is the Chief Product Officer at Think Bigger, where he leads product strategy and development for the tools that bring Sheena Iyengar's innovation methodology to leaders and organizations. His work connects innovation research with the practical decisions involved in building products people want to use.

With more than 25 years in product strategy and user experience design, Dan has helped shape more than 100 products — including the original strategy and user experience for Hulu, Citi Bike, Foursquare and Rent the Runway, plus work for The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Comcast and GE. His clients have also included Bloomberg, Blade, TD Ameritrade, the Kaiser Family Foundation and CNN, spanning media, financial services, transportation and health information.

He is the co-founder of the New York–based product strategy and design studios Hard Candy Shell and Charming Robot, co-author (with journalist Bob Sullivan) of the Audible Original The Barstool MBA, and host of the Story in a Bottle podcast.

An Audible Original

The Barstool MBA

There's a lot you can learn in a bar — and a lot you can learn from the bar business.

Co-authored with journalist Bob Sullivan, The Barstool MBA explores what running a bar can teach you about business. It draws on conversations with bar owners, managers, bartenders and industry insiders — looking beyond conventional business settings for lessons about customers, teams, and the decisions that keep a business running.

Speaking engagements

Dan reaches audiences through talks, workshops and university engagements on product strategy, user experience and building businesses. Drawing on more than 25 years of product and design work, he shares practical lessons about understanding customers, turning complex ideas into useful products, and moving from an idea to a business — at technology and design conferences across the US and internationally, including SXSW, DLD Conference, UX Poland, UX Copenhagen and Agile India, and at Google, Columbia Business School, Columbia Journalism School and the University of Virginia's Darden School of Business.
